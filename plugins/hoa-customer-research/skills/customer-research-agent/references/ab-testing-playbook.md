# Outreach A/B Testing Playbook

Use A/B testing to improve qualified customer-research outcomes, not merely email opens.

## Start only when

- qualification is consistent
- duplicate prevention works
- messages are accurate
- sent messages and replies are reliably logged
- enough comparable prospects exist

For fewer than 20 comparable sends per variant, treat results as directional.

## Test one variable at a time

Suitable variables include subject line, opening sentence, research framing, three email questions versus a 15-minute call, message length, offer to share findings, connection-note wording, and follow-up wording.

Do not simultaneously change persona, subject, body, channel, and call to action.

## Define before sending

- hypothesis
- variable tested
- Variant A
- Variant B
- target segment
- channel
- start date
- planned sample
- primary metric
- stop conditions

## Balance groups

Balance role category, company size, geography, ICP score, problem proximity, decision relevance, channel, and send timing.

## Metrics

Primary: qualified reply rate, meeting-booked rate, interview-completed rate, and useful-evidence rate.

Secondary: overall reply rate, positive reply rate, connection acceptance, referral, negative response, opt-out, and bounce rate.

Do not optimise primarily for open rate.

## Assignment

Save the variant in `Outreach.variant` before sending. Never switch the initial assignment after observing a response.

## Interpretation

Do not claim a winner from a small batch. Consider sample size, prospect quality, role mix, message accuracy, response quality, meeting conversion, negative responses, and operational mistakes.

## Prohibited tests

Do not test deceptive subjects, false urgency, false referrals, fabricated personalisation, misleading product claims, or hidden identity.
