# Email Templates

These are approved starting templates for customer research. Customise only with verified information and preserve the exact sent message in `Outreach.message`.

## Baseline — `BASELINE`

**Subject:** Researching HOA after-hours support

```text
Hi [First name],

I’m Ishaan, an AI engineer researching how HOA management companies handle resident calls and after-hours maintenance requests.

I reached out because [verified relevant reason].

I’m trying to understand the existing workflow before making product assumptions. Would you be open to answering three short questions by email or having a brief 15-minute conversation?

This is a research conversation, not a product demo. I’d also be happy to share an anonymised summary of what I learn.

Best,
Ishaan Gupta
```

## Rules

- Replace every placeholder.
- Do not send unresolved brackets.
- Use only verified facts.
- Do not imply a partnership or finished product.
- Do not attach files initially.
- Stop after two follow-ups by default.
- Stop immediately after any reply.
