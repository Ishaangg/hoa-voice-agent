# Competitive Landscape

## Market Structure and Major Management Firms

The competitive landscape is best understood in three layers: **market size**, **fragmentation/consolidation**, and **service-positioning by vendor type**. On market size, the cleanest official numbers are stock measures rather than revenue estimates: FCAR’s counts of associations, units, managers, management companies, and assessments provide the strongest primary picture. To estimate industry revenue, one must usually rely on company disclosures or market commentary that define the category differently. Consolidation is real, but the market is still far from concentrated. The Wall Street Journal reported in 2026 that the two largest players, **FirstService** and **Associa**, held only about **11%** of the market, and that private-equity-backed consolidation was still in an early stage. That is a crucial commercial fact: there are national platforms, but local and mid-market operators still matter enormously, and the market can re-fragment because barriers to entry remain relatively low. citeturn15news26
