# HOA and Community-Association Glossary

| Term | Working definition |
|---|---|
| HOA | Homeowners association; a private governing organisation for a common-interest residential community. |
| Community association | Umbrella term commonly covering homeowners associations, condominium associations, and cooperatives. |
| Board of directors | Volunteer-elected or developer-appointed governing body responsible for policy, budgets, oversight, and fiduciary decisions. |
| Management company | Third-party firm contracted to administer agreed financial, administrative, maintenance, communication, and compliance functions. |
| Community association manager (CAM) | Professional responsible for coordinating day-to-day association operations; licensing requirements vary by state. |
| Portfolio manager | Manager responsible for multiple communities rather than one dedicated on-site assignment. |
| On-site manager | Manager primarily dedicated to a specific community or property. |
| Self-managed association | Association whose board and volunteers perform most administration without a full-service management company. |
| CC&Rs | Covenants, Conditions, and Restrictions governing property use and owner obligations. |
| Governing documents | Declaration/CC&Rs, bylaws, articles, rules, resolutions, policies, and other documents defining association authority and procedures. |
| Assessment | Recurring or special amount charged to owners to fund association operations, reserves, or projects. |
| Reserve fund | Money designated for major repair and replacement of common assets. |
| Reserve study | Assessment of common-component condition, remaining useful life, replacement cost, and recommended funding. |
| ARC/ACC | Architectural Review Committee or Architectural Control Committee reviewing owner modification requests. |
| Violation | Alleged breach of governing documents or association rules. |
| Work order | Structured record of maintenance or service work requested, assigned, tracked, and closed. |
| Management agreement | Contract defining the management company's scope, fees, responsibilities, authority, and termination provisions. |
| Traditional management | Administrative, governance, and financial management model with many operational services outsourced. |
| Full-service management | Broader model that may include on-site staffing, engineering, maintenance, amenities, concierge/security, and ancillary services. |
| Large-scale community | Large, complex association often requiring on-site leadership and municipal-like services; definitions vary. |
| RFP | Request for proposal used to solicit comparable vendor or management-company bids. |
| Developer transition | Transfer of association control from the developer to homeowner governance. |
| Fiduciary duty | Board members' legal and ethical obligation to act in the association's interests under applicable law and documents. |

## Usage warning

Definitions are operational summaries. State law and individual governing documents may define terms differently.
