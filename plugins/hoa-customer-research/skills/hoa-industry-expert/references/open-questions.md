# Open Questions

## Voice-agent opportunity

- What is the average inbound call volume per community and per portfolio manager?
- What percentage of calls occur outside normal business hours?
- Which call categories account for most volume and handling time?
- What percentage can be resolved without account-specific data?
- What percentage require manager, board, vendor, counsel, or emergency-service escalation?
- What is the economic cost of current answering services and staff coverage?
- What error rate is acceptable for emergency classification?
- Which management platforms expose APIs for residents, work orders, balances, and documents?

## Buyer and economics

- Does the management company or association pay for communications automation?
- Which executive owns the budget at multi-community management firms?
- How does pricing need to vary by community, unit, call, or portfolio?
- What measurable outcome would justify purchase: fewer calls, lower answering-service spend, better SLAs, manager capacity, or retention?
- How long is the buying cycle for an adjunct workflow tool?
- Can a management company deploy centrally, or is approval required per association?

## Workflow and risk

- Which requests can be fully resolved without board authority?
- Which requests may be collected but never decided by an automated system?
- What state-specific call-recording, privacy, and CAM-licensing issues apply?
- What is the required escalation policy for emergencies and potential life-safety events?
- How are community-specific policies approved and versioned?
- What audit evidence do managers and boards require?

## Competitive validation

- Which HOA software vendors already offer native voice, conversational AI, or automated call handling?
- Which answering services specialise in community associations?
- Which management companies have built internal resident-contact centres?
- How do incumbent solutions price and integrate?
- Where do generic voice agents fail on HOA-specific authorisation and policy boundaries?

## Evidence status

The uploaded report explicitly notes gaps around portfolio/on-site prevalence and contains several time-sensitive market and competitor claims. Validate these before high-stakes product or investment decisions.
