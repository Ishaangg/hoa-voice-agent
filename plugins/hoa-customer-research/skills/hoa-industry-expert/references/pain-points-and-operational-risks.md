# Pain Points and Operational Risks

## Recurring Challenges

The most persistent pain points are operationally repetitive, politically sensitive, and hard to standardise across communities. Industry surveys and regulatory texts point to the same fault lines repeatedly: staffing, insurance, reserves, resident communications, vendor oversight, collections, and compliance. citeturn16view0turn20search17turn25view0turn26view0

Two observations matter especially for go-to-market.

First, the highest-value problems are usually **cross-functional** rather than departmental. Insurance is not just insurance; it touches budgets, reserve assumptions, vendor certificates, board approvals, resident notices, and claims documentation. Reserve planning is not just finance; it also touches engineering, procurement, owner communications, and statutory disclosures. Vendor oversight is not just maintenance; it is also compliance, risk, and board trust. citeturn26view0turn25view0turn35search7

Second, boards often feel these failures only after they become visible to residents. That means vendors that help the board and manager **spot issues earlier** gain commercial leverage. Examples include missed certificates of insurance, incomplete bid comparisons, overdue reserve updates, statusless work orders, inconsistent violation records, and untracked emergency escalations. These are operational “blind spots”, and they are exactly where third-party solutions tend to be most defensible. citeturn35search7turn22view3turn18view0
