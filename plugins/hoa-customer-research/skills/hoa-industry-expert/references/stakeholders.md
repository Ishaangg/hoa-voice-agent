# HOA Stakeholder Map

## Association board

**Primary role:** Governance, policy, budget approval, vendor/management oversight, and final approval for material decisions.

**Important boundary:** The board retains policy authority even when operations are delegated to a management company.

## Management company

**Primary role:** Executes day-to-day administrative, financial, maintenance, compliance, communication, and vendor-coordination workflows under contract.

**Commercial role:** Frequently recommends vendors and tools, but the association board may retain final approval depending on scope and spend.

## Community association manager

**Primary role:** Operational interface among the board, residents, vendors, accountants, legal counsel, reserve specialists, and insurers.

**Common operating models:** Portfolio manager across multiple communities or dedicated/on-site manager for larger communities.

## Homeowners and residents

**Primary role:** End users of association services and sources of requests, payments, complaints, maintenance reports, architectural requests, and governance pressure.

**Important distinction:** A resident is not always the legal owner; permissions and account access may differ.

## Vendors and contractors

Provide maintenance, landscaping, security, restoration, engineering, reserve-study, and other specialist services.

## Specialist advisers

- Association attorney: legal interpretation, disputes, collections, and procedural review.
- Reserve specialist or engineer: reserve studies and capital-planning input.
- Insurance broker or carrier: coverage, renewal, and claims expertise.
- Auditor, tax adviser, and banking partners: specialist financial support.

## Product buying roles

For every proposed product, explicitly identify:

- End user
- Operational champion
- Beneficiary
- Economic buyer
- Final approver
- Budget owner
- Security/legal reviewer
- Implementation owner
- Likely blocker
