# Knowledge Refresh Policy

## Stable knowledge — review annually

- Core terminology
- Stakeholder roles
- General governance structure
- Broad responsibility categories
- Historical development

## Moderately dynamic — review every 6 months

- Industry scale and statistics
- Management-company business models
- Common operating practices
- Buying and procurement patterns

## Dynamic — review every 30–90 days

- Competitor footprints
- Pricing and contract posture
- Product features and integrations
- Funding, acquisitions, and partnerships
- AI and automation capabilities
- Market consolidation claims

## Verify every time before high-stakes use

- Federal, state, and local laws
- Licensing requirements
- Call-recording and privacy rules
- Financial-handling requirements
- Emergency-response obligations
- Current prices
- Current API availability
- Current customer counts or executive leadership

## Update procedure

1. Preserve the prior claim in a dated report.
2. Record the new source, publication date, and access date.
3. Explain whether the claim was confirmed, narrowed, contradicted, or superseded.
4. Update the relevant reference only after adequate verification.
5. Change `last_verified` and retain important caveats.
6. Never turn vendor marketing into market-wide fact without corroboration.
